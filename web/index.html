    04 / THE MODEL

    Show the reasoning.

    P(X ≥ k) = 1 − Σj=0…k−1 e−D·f(D·f)j / j!

    D = mean mapped, retained depth; f = alternate-allele fraction; k = minimum alternate observations. The Poisson model represents ideal independent sampling at an accessible locus.

    Effective depth and raw yield are different quantities:

    raw Gb = D × genome Gb × samples
    ÷ (mapped fraction × retained fraction)
